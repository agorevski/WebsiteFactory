# Website Factory Suggestions

These suggestions focus on improving two outcomes:

1. **Generation effectiveness**: better factual capture, smarter planning, stronger QA, richer static output, and safer schema-first automation.
2. **Sales effectiveness**: clearer value framing, easier prospect review, trust-building artifacts, launch readiness, and repeatable sales packages.

## Highest-leverage priorities

| Priority | Suggestion | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Add a v2 content-to-Universal section adapter | Turns normalized facts into richer pages without duplicate hand-authored sections. |
| High | Make source provenance first-class | Makes generated claims safer, easier to review, and more credible to prospects. |
| High | Bridge generation plans to rendered output | Ensures marketplace selections become visible, sellable website sections. |
| High | Add proposal and review-room artifacts | Converts a preview into a sales-ready customer decision experience. |
| High | Add QA and local visibility scorecards | Turns existing validation/SEO tooling into customer-facing proof. |

## Suggested implementation sequence

1. **Trust and readiness foundation**: source provenance, content completeness scoring, citation/fact sheets, and customer-facing QA scorecards.
2. **Generation quality upgrades**: v2-to-Universal adapters, smarter section/page planning, and broader render adapters.
3. **Sales packaging**: proposal artifacts, review room, variation comparison, deployment readiness packet, and pricing tiers.
4. **Automation loop**: AI pipeline build/QA/repair steps, customer feedback-to-YAML tasks, generated service/location routes, and vertical sales bundles.
